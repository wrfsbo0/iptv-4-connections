## What about streaming quality?

TiviLive advertises support for 4K, UHD and FHD, but the actual experience still depends on the device, network connection and the individual stream.

Its installation guidance says buffering can be related to the user's internet connection and recommends practical fixes such as using Ethernet instead of Wi-Fi, restarting the router, closing other bandwidth-heavy apps, and adjusting stream quality when necessary.

The service's terms also state that it does not guarantee 100% uptime and that service interruptions can occur because of maintenance, upstream providers, or connection issues. The same terms recommend at least 10 Mbps for HD streaming and 25 Mbps for 4K content.

For a 4-connection household, that means the internet setup matters just as much as the subscription. Four streams are four streams. The router does not get a vote.
